## 2. Micro-Dialogues for Both Tracks (حوارات مصغرة للمحادثة)

### Dialogue 1: Greetings & Identity (التحية والتعارف)

#### 🌍 General Track Version

* **A:** السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ. (*Peace be upon you.*)
* **B:** وَعَلَيْكُمُ السَّلَامُ وَرَحْمَةُ اللهِ. (*And upon you be peace and Allah's mercy.*)
* **A:** مَنِ اسْمُكَ؟ (*What is your name?*)
* **B:** اسْمِي سَمِيرٌ، وَأَنَا مُعَلِّمٌ جَدِيدٌ. وَأَنْتَ؟ (*My name is Samir, and I am a new teacher. And you?*)
* **A:** أَنَا دَانِيَال، أَنَا طَالِبٌ هُنَا. (*I am Daniel, I am a student here.*)

#### 🕌 Quranic / Faith Track Version

* **A:** السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ يَا أَخِي. (*Peace be upon you, my brother.*)
* **B:** وَعَلَيْكُمُ السَّلَامُ وَرَحْمَةُ اللهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ. (*And upon you be peace, Allah's mercy and blessings.*)
* **A:** هَلْ أَنْتَ ذَاهِبٌ إِلَى الْمَسْجِدِ؟ (*Are you going to the mosque?*)
* **B:** نَعَمْ، أَنَا ذَاهِبٌ لِصَلَاةِ الظُّهْرِ إِنْ شَاءَ اللهُ. (*Yes, I am going for the Dhuhr prayer, God willing.*)
* **A:** جَزَاكَ اللهُ خَيْراً، أَنَا قَادِمٌ مَعَكَ. (*May Allah reward you with goodness, I am coming with you.*)

## Review notes (before ingestion) — added by Claude, 2026-09-22

This pack is good raw material, but it is a **source document**, not ingestable content yet. What
ingestion requires:

1. **Schema mapping.** The drills use `track` and `type`; the app uses `emphasisTag`
   (`general|quranic|both`) and the exercise `kind` registry. `pattern_substitution` → `choice`;
   `sentence_builder` → `build` with `acceptedOrders` (needs M28.3); `fill_in_blank` has **no**
   registered kind — it would map to `cloze` or `choice`, or need a new kind (its own scope).
2. **De-duplication.** Many entries already exist as lexemes (بَيْت `lex:hom-01`, مَسْجِد
   `lex:pla-04`, مَدْرَسَة, سُوق, أَب, أُم, أَخ, أُخت, صَدِيق, كَبِير, صَغِير, قَرِيب, بَعِيد, …). Ingestion
   must reference existing ids and add `emphasisTag` to them, not create duplicates — exactly how
   the M28 minimal-pairs reused existing lexemes.
3. **Linguistic verification (standing rule 1).** Every new string checked against a reference for
   spelling, ḥarakāt, and level-fit before it enters `content/`. A couple to confirm during that
   pass: صَبَاحاً / خَيْراً are written with a final tanwīn-alif (fine), and مِحْرَاب / بُقْعَة /
   ضَلَال بَعِيد sit at the higher end of A2 register.
4. **Level placement.** New vocab/dialogues/adjectives belong to A1.2 (app A1) and A2 units, not the
   A0 units M28.1 just filled. They ride on the existing curriculum spine.
5. **Copyright.** The Arabic here is original example sentences (not copyrighted translations), so it
   is usable — the standing copyright constraint is specifically about published EN/FR Quran
   translations and named reciters' audio, which this pack does not include.
6. **Emphasis reachability (M28 invariant).** `general`-only and `quranic`-only items must not be the
   sole carrier of any objective; each needs a `both`/opposite counterpart in its unit so every
   learner can complete the unit. The build validator enforces this once split content lands.

Recommended path: fold this into the **A1.2 content batch** that follows M28.2/M28.3 (the drills need
M28.3's `acceptedOrders` grading and the emphasis machinery), authored through the normal scope-doc +
verification workflow.
